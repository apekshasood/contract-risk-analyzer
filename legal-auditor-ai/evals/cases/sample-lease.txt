RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (SAMPLE, WRITTEN FOR TESTING)

1. Term. This lease starts on 1 January 2027 and runs for 11 months.
2. Rent. The Tenant shall pay rent of Rs 25,000 on the 1st of each month.
3. Rent increase. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time by giving 7 days notice.
4. Security deposit. The Tenant shall pay a deposit of Rs 1,50,000, which the Landlord may keep in full if the Tenant leaves before the end of the term.
5. Late payment. A late fee of Rs 1,000 per day applies to any rent paid after the 1st.
6. Repairs. The Tenant shall pay for all repairs to the property, including structural repairs and repairs to the roof.
7. Entry. The Landlord may enter the property at any time without notice.
8. Termination. The Landlord may end this lease with 15 days notice. The Tenant may not end this lease early.
9. Utilities. The Tenant shall pay electricity and water bills.
10. Disputes. Any dispute shall be decided only by an arbitrator chosen by the Landlord.
